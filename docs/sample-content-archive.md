# NoorQuest Sample Content

## 1. Halal Scanner Game Dialogues (Lumi the Lantern)

**Correct Halal Item (e.g., Apple):**
Lumi: "Yay! Apples are Halal and super yummy! Allah gave us fruits to enjoy and stay healthy. ⭐"

**Haram Item (e.g., Pork):**
Lumi: "Oh, this has pork. We don't eat pork because Allah knows what's best for our bodies and hearts. Let's choose something else! 🥕"

**Mushbooh (e.g., Gelatin candy):**
Lumi: "Hmm, this might have doubtful ingredients. Let's ask Mom or Dad! That's a smart choice."

**Encouragement on streak:**
Lumi: "You're becoming a real Halal Hero! Keep going, my friend!"

## 3. Story Script: "The Honest Prophet" (Short Story for Story Grove)

**Title:** Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and the Trust

Once upon a time in Makkah, a man gave the Prophet a beautiful treasure to keep safe. Many years later, when the man returned, the Prophet gave it back without a single coin missing.

The Prophet said: "I am the most honest person. A Muslim must always tell the truth and keep promises."

**Interactive Question for Child:**
"What would you do if someone gave you their toy to keep? Would you return it nicely?"

**Moral:** Honesty makes our hearts bright like Noor.

## 4. Parent Guide: "Explaining Halal to Your 7-Year-Old"

**Key Talking Points:**
- Halal means "good and allowed" – like choosing healthy snacks.
- Allah loves us and gives us rules to protect us, just like we tell our kids not to touch hot stoves.
- Example: "We don't eat pork because it's not clean for our bodies, like how we avoid dirty water."

**Activity Idea:**
Go to the grocery store together. Let your child find 3 items with a Halal symbol and talk about why it's special.

**Common Challenge:**
Child wants candy at a party → "Some foods are for everyone, but we have special choices that help us remember Allah. Let's pick our own treat at home!"
