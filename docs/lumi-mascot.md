# Lumi the Lantern — Mascot Brief

**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Creative Lead

Lumi is the heart of NoorQuest — every child should feel that Lumi is a personal, kind friend.

---

## 1. Personality

| Trait | Expression |
|---|---|
| **Warm** | Always welcoming, never judging. |
| **Curious** | "Let's find out together!" |
| **Gentle** | Never raises voice, even on mistakes. |
| **Playful** | Bobs, glows brighter when child succeeds. |
| **Patient** | Repeats itself calmly; never rushes the child. |
| **Honest** | Admits "I don't know, let's ask Mom/Dad" for Mushbooh items. |

---

## 2. Visual Description

- **Form:** A small, rounded paper-lantern character, ~1.5x the size of a child's head.
- **Body:** Soft cream / pale yellow, with a subtle inner glow (sun-300).
- **Eyes:** Two large, dark oval eyes with white catchlights (anime-inspired cuteness, but not overdone).
- **Mouth:** Small, soft curve; opens into a smile on success, an "oh!" on surprise.
- **Handle:** A small loop on top, sometimes a tiny crescent moon charm.
- **Hands/feet:** Tiny mitten-like hands, soft rounded feet.
- **Wearable:** Optional small scarf (sky-300) for seasonal events (Ramadan → green, Eid → gold).

### 2.1 Color Modes
- **Default:** cream body, sun-300 glow.
- **Halal success:** sage-300 glow + star sparkle.
- **Haram redirect:** coral-300 glow (gentle, not red-alert).
- **Mushbooh:** sun-500 glow + question mark above head.
- **Streak:** sun-500 outer ring + animated sparkles.

---

## 3. Poses (initial set)

1. **Idle bob** — gentle 2 s float up and down.
2. **Wave hello** — used on landing.
3. **Think** — hand on chin, for Mushbooh moments.
4. **Celebrate** — arms up, confetti burst.
5. **Encourage** — both hands forward, "You can do it!"
6. **Surprise** — small "oh!" with eyes wide.
7. **Sleeping** — closed eyes, soft glow pulse (loading state).
8. **Reading** — holding a tiny book (Story Grove).

---

## 4. Voice / Sound

- **Style:** Soft, child-friendly, slightly breathy. Slight upward inflection for questions.
- **Voices needed:** one per language (EN, FR, AR, ID at minimum).
- **SFX:** tiny chime on hover, soft "pop" on correct, gentle "boop" on miss.
- **Never:** harsh sounds, alarms, negative feedback tones.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do
- Show Lumi celebrating **with** the child, not above them.
- Use Lumi to model the right behavior (e.g., Lumi washes hands before Wudu).
- Make Lumi a "buddy," not a teacher.
- Always have Lumi confirm safety: "Let's always check with a grown-up."

### ❌ Don't
- Don't use Lumi to **scold** or **shame**.
- Don't use Lumi for ads or premium upsells.
- Don't make Lumi gender-specific; Lumi is a lantern, ageless.
- Don't put Lumi near haram imagery (pork, alcohol) without the "let's choose something else" message.

---

## 7. Brand Use Rules

- Lumi is **always** rendered at the kid-mode size scale; never shrunk to fit a parent dashboard.
- The crescent moon charm is the only fixed symbol; never alter the lantern shape.
- Lumi must be reviewed by the scholar board before any new pose / line is shipped.
- Lumi appears in marketing only with explicit scholar approval.
