## Module 4: Salah (full breakdown)

### 4.1 Wudu Steps (8 steps, kid language)
1. **Bismillah!** "We start in the name of Allah."
2. **Wash hands 3 times** — "Splash splash!"
3. **Rinse mouth 3 times** — gentle swish.
4. **Clean nose 3 times** — soft sniff.
5. **Wash face 3 times** — forehead to chin.
6. **Wash arms to elbows 3 times** — right then left.
7. **Wipe head once** — from front to back.
8. **Wash feet to ankles 3 times** — right then left.

**Closing:** "Now you are ready to stand before Allah in Salah. Takbir! 🤲"

### 4.2 Salah Positions (Fajr — 2 rak'at example)
- **Takbir:** "Allahu Akbar" — hands to ears.
- **Qiyam:** standing, hands folded, Surah Al-Fatiha + small Surah.
- **Ruku:** bowing, "Subhana Rabbiyal Adheem" x3.
- **Sujud:** forehead on ground, "Subhana Rabbiyal A'la" x3.
- **Tashahhud:** sitting, finger raised, shahada.
- **Salam:** "Assalamu alaikum" right then left.

Positive reinforcement: "MashaAllah! You did the steps perfectly. Allah loves when we pray with a clean heart and body."

## Module 5: Prophet Stories (3 full scripts)

### Story 1: The Honest Trader
**Title:** Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and the Trust
**Setting:** Makkah, long ago.

> Once upon a time, a man gave the Prophet ﷺ a beautiful treasure to keep safe. Many years later, when the man returned, the Prophet ﷺ gave it back — every single coin.  
> The Prophet ﷺ said: "I am the most honest person. A Muslim must always tell the truth and keep promises."

**Interactive question:** "What would you do if someone gave you their favorite toy to keep?"  
**Moral:** Honesty makes our hearts bright like Noor.

### Story 2: The Kind Neighbor
**Title:** Prophet Yusuf (AS) and His Brothers
**Setting:** Ancient Canaan.

> Young Yusuf's brothers were jealous. Instead of hurting him, his father Yaqub taught him patience. Years later, Yusuf became a wise leader in Egypt. When his brothers came hungry, he forgave them and shared his food.

**Interactive question:** "How can you be kind to a brother or sister today?"  
**Moral:** Patience and kindness turn enemies into family.

### Story 3: The Patient Scholar
**Title:** Prophet Ayyub (AS) and the Storm
**Setting:** Ancient land of Uz.

> Prophet Ayyub lost his health, family, and wealth — but he never stopped saying "Alhamdulillah." After many years of patience, Allah restored everything to him, doubled.

**Interactive question:** "When something is hard, what dua can we say?"  
**Moral:** Patience is powerful. Allah hears every patient heart.

## Module 7: Parent Guide (selected excerpts)

### "Explaining Halal to Your 7-Year-Old"
- **Key talking points:** "Halal means good and allowed — like choosing healthy snacks." "Allah loves us and gives us rules to protect us, just like we tell our kids not to touch hot stoves."
- **Example:** "We don't eat pork because it's not clean for our bodies, like how we avoid dirty water."
- **Activity:** Go to the grocery store. Let your child find 3 items with a Halal symbol and talk about why it's special.
- **Common challenge:** Child wants candy at a party → "Some foods are for everyone, but we have special choices that help us remember Allah. Let's pick our own treat at home!"

### Discussion Starters
- "What was your favorite thing you learned with Lumi today?"
- "How can we be kind like the Prophets?"
- "What Halal food should we make this weekend?"
